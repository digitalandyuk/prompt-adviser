# Repeatable workflow prompts

Use for prompts that convert an existing conversation into a reusable production process.

## Extract the brief

- Separate goals, accepted preferences, approvals, rejected approaches and outstanding questions.
- Preserve user decisions; do not turn incidental earlier defaults into permanent requirements.
- Replace trip names, dates, accounts, quantities and budgets with explicit inputs where reuse requires them.
- Avoid carrying unrelated personal information or private conversation excerpts into public searches or external services.

## Sequence the work

- Verify source access and tool capabilities before promising delivery.
- Inventory inputs and assign stable identifiers before selection or layout.
- Put selection, specification and representative prototypes before full production.
- Batch material questions and keep authorised independent work moving.
- Specify exactly which milestones require feedback; avoid both endless confirmations and ambiguous unattended execution.
- Separate previews, editable masters and final exported files. A flattened preview is not an editable master.
- Keep originals, selections, exclusions and revision history recoverable. Define export/import of choices and verify round trips where practical.
- Save resumable state durably; do not assume a temporary path or another conversation will remain accessible.

## Define evidence and completion

- Tie each stage to a concrete result, not an attempted tool action.
- Track selected items through final placement, including unplaced or repeated items.
- Distinguish automated screening from visual or human judgement; flag uncertainty.
- Verify mutable prices, specifications, discounts and deadlines at execution time. Separate quantities, unit price, total, taxes and delivery.
- Use provider-specific templates and requirements instead of reusing old dimensions blindly.
- Define a final check in the receiving service where possible, with an honest fallback if access is unavailable.
- Keep payment, production release and other consequential commitments behind the user's stated boundary and applicable permissions.

## Keep the prompt usable

State outcome, inputs, autonomy, review milestones, deliverables and acceptance criteria. Include only procedures that prevent observed failures. Prefer a compact core with conditional detail over a long universal checklist. Keep optional paid APIs and delegation opt-in. Explain only material trade-offs; do not promise unmeasured time, cost or quality gains.
